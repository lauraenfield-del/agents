# Code of Conduct

This repository aims to be welcoming, professional, and safe for contributors of all experience levels.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements
- give feedback about the work, not the person
- be open to correction and collaboration
- help keep discussions focused, civil, and inclusive

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not allowed:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- personal attacks, insults, or hostile language
- deliberate disruption, trolling, or bad-faith engagement
- sharing private or sensitive information without permission
- behavior that makes participation unsafe or unreasonably difficult for others

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainer privately instead of escalating in public threads when possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, hide, or remove content, and may limit participation from contributors whose behavior harms the project or its community.
